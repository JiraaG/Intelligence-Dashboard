# Riassunto

Female workers have filed a class action lawsuit against Fortescue alleging systemic sexual harassment and violence at remote iron ore mines in Western Australia. The case follows similar legal actions against BHP and Rio Tinto, highlighting cultural failures within the strategic mining sector.
