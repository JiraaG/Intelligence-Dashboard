# Riassunto

Pakistan is leveraging its role as a peacemaker between the US and Iran to improve its diplomatic standing and seek economic relief from Washington and Gulf allies. The mediation aims to prevent regional conflict spillover and stabilize energy markets affected by the closure of the Strait of Hormuz.
