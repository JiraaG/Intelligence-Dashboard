# Riassunto

Iran's IRGC Navy has threatened action against vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz without Tehran's approval, asserting operational control over the critical energy artery. This occurs despite a US-Iran memorandum of understanding and US Treasury sanctions against Iran's Persian Gulf Strait Authority.
