# Riassunto

Extreme heatwaves in Rennes, France, have forced Cogent Communications to recommend an orderly shutdown of customer equipment to prevent hardware damage. The facility's cooling systems were unable to maintain operational temperatures, highlighting vulnerabilities in digital infrastructure to extreme weather.
