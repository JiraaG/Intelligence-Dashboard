# Riassunto

Immigrant communities in Minnesota continue to experience trauma and economic instability following the federal 'Operation Metro Surge' immigration crackdown. The operation, which involved mass detentions and resulted in the deaths of two US citizens, has left lasting social scars despite the withdrawal of most federal agents.
