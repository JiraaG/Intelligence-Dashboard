# Riassunto

South Korea's IPO activity has plummeted due to the structural dominance of Chaebols and governance issues, hindering the emergence of new independent companies. The government is implementing the 'Corporate value-up initiative' to combat the 'Korea discount' and improve minority shareholder protection.
