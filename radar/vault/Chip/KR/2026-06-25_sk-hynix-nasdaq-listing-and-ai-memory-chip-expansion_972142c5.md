# Riassunto

SK Hynix plans to raise up to $29.4 billion through a Nasdaq listing to expand its investor base and fund AI memory chip production. The company is investing in the Yongin semiconductor cluster in South Korea and a new $4 billion packaging plant in Indiana, USA.

# Entità Infrastrutturali

- Yongin semiconductor cluster
- Indiana advanced chip-packaging plant
