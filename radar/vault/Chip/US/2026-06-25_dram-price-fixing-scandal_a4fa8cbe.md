# Riassunto

Major global semiconductor manufacturers, including Samsung and Hynix, were fined hundreds of millions of dollars for an international DRAM price-fixing conspiracy. The scandal highlighted the vulnerability of the global electronics supply chain to cartel-like behavior among key memory chip producers.
